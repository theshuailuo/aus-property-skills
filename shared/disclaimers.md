# 免责句库 · Disclaimer library

每个 skill 的报告末尾从这里取句子:**通用句 + 本 skill 涉及的领域句**。不要自己另写。
Every skill report ends with the **general line + the line(s) for each domain it touches**. Do not improvise new wording.

## 写法原则 · Tone rules

1. 积极、不过度:说清这是工具、建议找谁、自行决定的后果由谁承担。不吓人,不每页水印。
2. **不写成许可**:不能出现读起来像「可以不找专业人士」的句子。
3. 不承诺结果:不写「保证」「稳赚」「一定涨」。
4. 语言跟随用户;中英都要时,中文在前。

禁用写法示例(校验脚本会拦,本行带 BANNED-EXAMPLE 标记所以不报错):「您可以不经律师自行签署」「这份清单够用,无需律师」 <!-- BANNED-EXAMPLE -->

## 通用 · General(每份报告都放)

- 中:这是我们自己买房时会用的工具,仅供参考。本报告是一般信息,未结合您的个人情况。
- EN: This is a tool we use in our own property purchases, provided for reference only. It is general information and does not take your personal circumstances into account.

## 法律 / 合同 · Legal & contracts

- 中:涉及合同条款,建议您请律师或产权转让师(conveyancer)审阅后再签署。如您未经专业人士审阅自行决定,相关后果由您自行承担。
- EN: For anything involving contract terms, we recommend having a solicitor or conveyancer review it before you sign. If you decide without professional review, you accept the consequences of that decision.
- 法律文件以英文原文为准,中文译文仅供理解。/ The English text of any legal document prevails; Chinese translations are for understanding only.

## 贷款 · Lending

- 中:借贷测算只是示意,以银行或持牌贷款经纪(credit licensee)的正式评估为准。建议您在做决定前咨询持牌专业人士;如您未经专业人士审阅自行决定,相关后果由您自行承担。
- EN: Borrowing figures are illustrative only; a lender's or licensed broker's formal assessment is what counts. We recommend speaking with a licensed professional before deciding; if you decide without professional review, you accept the consequences.

## 税务 · Tax

- 中:税务部分是一般信息与示例算法,以 ATO 与各州税务局的现行规定为准。建议您咨询注册税务代理(registered tax agent)或会计;如您未经专业人士审阅自行决定,相关后果由您自行承担。
- EN: Tax content is general information with worked examples; current ATO and state revenue office rules prevail. We recommend consulting a registered tax agent or accountant; if you decide without professional review, you accept the consequences.

## 投资 · Investment

- 中:本报告不构成个人投资建议。具体请自行分析或寻求持牌专业人士意见。
- EN: This report is not personal investment advice. Please do your own analysis or seek advice from a licensed professional.
- 无买家档案时追加 / when no buyer profile exists, add:「未结合您的实际情况,具体请自行分析或寻求专业人士意见。」/ "This does not reflect your actual circumstances; please do your own analysis or seek professional advice."
